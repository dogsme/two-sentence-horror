An entry for [make.fun's Two-Sentence Horror bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253): the scariest horror story told in exactly two sentences.

## The entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13) writes one horror story of exactly two sentences to `/output/entry.txt`. The first sentence is a small comfort — a parent finally sleeping because the baby monitor lets them hear their son breathing in the next room. The second sentence takes that comfort away: the monitor has been dead for days, and the son has not been in the house at all. Nothing in the story says what was breathing; the reader has to supply that.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The story is written to `/output/entry.txt` as UTF-8 text, two sentences, well under the 400-character limit.
